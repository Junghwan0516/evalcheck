Evaluation protocol

1,800 training / 600 development / 600 test records. Lexical connected components are kept within one partition, with threshold 0.8 and a complete three-way audit returning zero overlap rows. Vocabulary and IDF are fit on training text only.

A local analysis plan was written before fitting, then a model-selection lock was saved before final test predictions. This was not an externally preregistered or independently blinded study.

Resampling unit: 591 test components; 5,000 paired replicates. The interval conditions on the fitted models and this split.

What the result supports

The selected representation has a higher observed Macro-F1 score. The interval includes zero; a clear positive difference has not been established. This does not demonstrate an accuracy benefit caused by the auditing tool.

  • One public dataset and one split; no independent replication.
  • Improvement, if observed, compares feature representations and does not estimate the causal effect of EvalCheck.
  • The corpus was previously inspected for audit examples; test labels were not used to choose candidates, hyperparameters or a split seed in this experiment.
  • Grouping uses all input text for a predeclared unlabeled isolation rule; TF-IDF vocabularies and IDF are fit on training text only.
  • Bootstrap conditions on fitted models and selected candidate; it is not a full uncertainty estimate for retraining or deploying on a new population.
