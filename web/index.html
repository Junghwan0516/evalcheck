Coverage and limitations

    Evidence you can reproduce

    A fixed synthetic negative control exposed inflated evaluation scores: 79.5% with leaked feature selection vs 51.6% with a training-only Pipeline, averaged across ten noise datasets. This is not a real-world effect estimate.

    Detection, with its limits included

    On 40 authored cases: 20 risks detected, 4 missed, 16 safe cases unflagged. Unsupported risks remain in overall recall. This is not independent real-world validation.
